AI writer
AI writing is off. Add your free Gemini API key in Settings. The graphics editor, checks and export work without it.

App brief

Describe your app once. Every title, description, caption and graphic is built from this brief, so list only features the app really has.

Brand look

Used by the icon, feature graphic and all screenshots.

Competitors

Add up to five top-ranked apps for your main keyword. Gemini can search for them and read their Play Store pages from the links. If a page cannot be read, open it, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste the text.

Listing text

Title, short description and full description, written from your brief and the competitor keywords. Edit anything. Counters and checks follow Google Play limits and metadata policy.

Edit with AI

Pick a field, say what to change, press Apply. Undo brings back the previous text.

    App icon

    Google Play wants a full square with no rounded corners and no shadow. Play rounds the corners itself, shown in the masked previews.

    512 x 512 px · 32-bit PNG · max 1024 KB · full square, Play applies the mask

    Feature graphic

    The banner Play shows as your video cover and in large promotional placements. Keep the message short and away from the edges.

    Drag the text or the phone on the banner to move it.

    1024 x 500 px · JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha · exported here as JPEG

    Screenshots

    Upload at least 6 real captures from your app. Select any slot to edit its text, sizes, colors and layout, then export for phone, 7-inch and 10-inch tablet.

    Templates

    One click restyles every slot. Captions, images and added elements stay.

    Slots

    Select a slot to edit it. 6 minimum, 8 maximum.

    Editor

    Drag the text or the device on the preview to move it.

    Extra elements

    For tablet sets Google prefers captures taken on a tablet layout. If your app has a tablet UI, upload those captures and export the tablet sets from them.

    Languages

    Translate the whole listing into any Play Store language. Switch the editing language from the menu on the left at any time: the listing text, every screenshot caption and the feature graphic follow it, and you can fine-tune each language by hand. Layout, colors and images are shared. Each language exports to its own folder.

    Check and export

    Everything Play Console asks for on the main store listing page, checked against Google's limits.

      Open Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey)

      The key stays in this browser and is sent only to Google. If a model says its quota is used up, pick another one here; Flash-Lite models usually have the highest free limits.
